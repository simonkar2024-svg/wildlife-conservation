Wildlife Guardians Kenya

Wildlife Guardians in Kenya are individuals or groups of people who play a big role in protecting our country's Biodiversity. They include the following;

  • Kenya Wildlife Service Rangers
  • Lions Guardians and Community Programs
  • Orphan Sanctuaries and Keepers
  • Wildlife Veterinarians and Women Guardians

All above work under an umbrella called Wildlife Guardian Trust, which is guided by below Mission and Vision Statments.

Our Mission

To protect wildlife and restore natural habitats through community participation, conservation awareness and responsible action.

Our Vision

A future where wildlife flourishes in healthy natural habitats and local communities are respected, empowered and actively involved in conservation.
